# Reproducible Research

Reproducible research makes methods, data, and computational steps clear enough
for other people to understand and repeat the work. Begin by documenting the
research question, inputs, software environment, and analysis decisions.

Version control, dependency records, tests, and durable documentation make it
easier to collaborate and revisit a project later.

Source: The Turing Way. This short fixture is provided for offline development
and is not a replacement for the complete book.
